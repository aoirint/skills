# English Writing Guidance

## Developer documentation

Google's developer documentation guide provides practical guidance for technical
English. Its pages on [articles](https://developers.google.com/style/articles)
and [product names](https://developers.google.com/style/product-names) help
separate grammatical noun phrases from naming conventions. Its
[voice guidance](https://developers.google.com/style/voice) explains when active
voice improves responsibility and when passive voice remains useful.

[Writing for a global audience](https://developers.google.com/style/translation)
is useful when considering modifier scope, noun strings, terminology, and readers
who use English as an additional language. These are editorial guidelines, not
universal grammatical laws. Do not impose Google's US English, product-specific
rules, or numerical limits on unrelated writing; follow the destination's
conventions and the meaning of the passage.

## Sentence relationships

Purdue University's writing lab explains
[parallel structure](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/mechanics/parallel_structure.html)
through grammatical relationships within coordinated expressions. Consult it
when a list or sentence mixes incompatible forms. Parallel syntax should clarify
the relationship between ideas, not erase differences in their meaning.
